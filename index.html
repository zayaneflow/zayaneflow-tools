Two free tools for cold email agencies. The first one plans how many booked meetings a client needs. The second one prices each meeting. Type three numbers while they talk, then share your screen.

See the mailboxes, leads and meetings it takes to hit a revenue goal, and what lands in the first 60 days. Press Play and the camera walks your prospect through every step.

How to use them on a call

  1. Ask three numbers.Their extra revenue goal for the next 12 months, their average project price, and their close rate.
  2. Type them in while they talk.The planner shows the meetings they need and what's in the pipeline by day 60.
  3. Share your screen and press Play.Then open the pricer: it shows your price per meeting and what they keep on every signed project.

Made by Zayane (Zayaneflow). Free for anyone. Source code on GitHub.

The numbers are estimates based on cold email averages: about 1% interested replies, 1 in 3 of those booking a call. Your real campaign numbers replace them after the first weeks of sending. The light versions skip the 3D view and load fast on any phone.
